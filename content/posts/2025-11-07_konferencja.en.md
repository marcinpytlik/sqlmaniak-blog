On December 4, 2025, I will speak on the **Main Stage at DevAI 2025**. This is where monitoring, data, and generative AI come together in a practical scenario for every DBA.

My talk is titled:

**Telemetry and Generative AI: Building a Digital DBA Assistant
How data from Telegraf, InfluxDB, and SQL Server can power an intelligent recommendation system**

---

### 🔍 What the talk will cover

Over 35 minutes, we will explore how telemetry can become more than a collection of metrics: it can fuel an intelligent assistant for SQL Server administrators.

I will demonstrate:
- how to collect data from SQL Server, the operating system, and services,
- how to model it in InfluxDB,
- how a data pipeline can feed a recommendation system,
- how generative AI can assist with diagnosis and suggestions,
- what a prototype digital DBA assistant looks like.

No marketing, no slide filler — just practical examples.

### 🧠 Who is it for?

Administrators, developers, and engineers who want to see how telemetry, monitoring, and generative AI can work together — not as a novelty, but as practical support for everyday SQL Server management.

### 🔗 Why this topic?

Because the future of DBA work is not only about reacting quickly. It is about systems that **understand context**, detect patterns, anticipate problems, and suggest solutions before an incident occurs.

This does not replace the administrator. It makes the administrator more effective.
